# Counting at the festival — what to do

Thank you for helping. This takes about a minute to learn.

**Scan the QR code on the card taped to your post.** Two big buttons appear on your
phone. Keep the phone in your hand and the screen on.

**Tap the green IN button once for every person who walks past you in the direction
the card says.** Tap the red OUT button once for every person going the other way. The
card at your post spells out which direction is which.

Groups: tap once per person. A family of four is four taps.

**If you tap twice by accident, press UNDO.** It removes your last tap. It only works
for a minute, so use it right away. If you miss a few people, don't worry and don't
try to catch up — just keep counting from now.

**If something's wrong, press FLAG** and pick what it is: long line, spill or hazard,
needs staff, or medical. The organizer sees it immediately. For a real emergency, use
your radio or phone first — flag it afterwards.

**Don't worry about signal.** The app keeps working with no bars. Your taps are saved
on your phone and sent automatically when service comes back. If the dot at the top
right is amber and says "waiting", that is normal and everything is fine. Just keep
tapping.

**Please don't put the phone in your pocket.** If the screen is off, you're not
counting, and the organizer's numbers go wrong.

Questions during the event? Find the organizer. Thank you again.
